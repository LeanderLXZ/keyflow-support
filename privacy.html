How Keyflow handles text, local storage, permissions, and support.

Effective date: November 17, 2026

Keyflow processes editor text on your Mac. It does not send your editor text, History, or Favorites to our servers. Private Mode skips History entries; it still saves your draft locally.

Xuzheng Lu provides Keyflow, a macOS utility that turns text you enter into keyboard events sent to an application you choose.

Information handled by the app

Keyflow processes your editor text on your Mac. It does not operate an account service or send your editor text, History, or Favorites to our servers. The current app includes no advertising, third-party analytics, or tracking service.

When you choose Send, Keyflow delivers keyboard events to the destination application. That application, and any remote service it connects to, handles the resulting text under its own practices. When you choose Copy, text is placed on the system clipboard, where macOS clipboard features and other applications may access it.

Local storage and retention

Keyflow stores an editor recovery buffer as buffer.txt, History and Favorites in history.sqlite, and preferences through macOS. Settings > Data shows the data folder used by your installed version; you can choose another folder there. A selected cloud-synced or network folder may be copied or transmitted by the service responsible for that folder. Keyflow does not provide its own cloud synchronization.

The editor buffer is saved while you work. It can support recovery after an unexpected exit. Normal quitting attempts to clear the recovery buffer. A completed editor send clears an unchanged buffer after the required storage operations succeed; interrupted or failed sends preserve the original text.

History retention follows your settings. Favorites keep independent copies and remain when History is cleared or pruned. You can remove entries individually or clear History and Favorites separately in Settings.

Private Mode prevents new History entries. It does not disable local draft storage, erase existing History or Favorites, or make the destination app private.

Local text storage does not have additional encryption provided by Keyflow. Protection depends on your Mac, account permissions, disk encryption and the storage location you select. Deletion is logical removal, not a promise of forensic erasure; backups, filesystem snapshots or database pages may retain copies.

Permissions and diagnostics

Keyflow requests macOS Accessibility permission to send keystrokes. It observes the active application's identity and focus state to route sends and stop when the destination changes. You can revoke permission in System Settings > Privacy & Security > Accessibility; sending will then be unavailable.

The current app writes local macOS diagnostic messages about focus and window state, including foreground application names and process identifiers. These messages do not contain the editor text. Keyflow does not upload them to us. Retention of these logs is managed by macOS. Private Mode does not disable them. Operating-system diagnostics and backups are controlled separately by your system settings.

Your choices

You can clear the editor, disable History, use Private Mode, delete saved entries, clear History and Favorites separately, change the data folder, or revoke Accessibility permission. Reset Settings does not delete History, Favorites or editor text.

To remove local app data, first note the selected data folder, quit Keyflow, and remove its data files from that folder. Copies in backups or synced storage need to be managed with those services. Removing the app alone may leave its local data and preferences on your Mac.

Support

If you contact us, we receive the information you choose to include, such as your email address and problem description. Support email is hosted by Google Gmail, which processes correspondence under its own terms and privacy policy. Do not send confidential text or unredacted History files. We use support information to respond and investigate, and retain it only as needed for that purpose or applicable obligations. Contact us to request deletion of support correspondence we control. We cannot delete data held by a destination app or your backup provider.

This website

This support website is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub logs visitors' IP addresses for security purposes. See the GitHub Privacy Statement for the hosting provider's practices. We do not add analytics, advertising, tracking scripts, cookies, or contact forms to this website. An email link opens your email application; it does not send a message automatically.

Changes and contact

We will update this policy if Keyflow's data practices change. The effective date above identifies the current policy.
